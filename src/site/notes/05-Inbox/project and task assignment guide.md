## Introduction

At NASCOMSOFT EMBEDDED LTD, effective task management is essential to delivering quality work, meeting deadlines, and maintaining customer satisfaction.

The company uses a centralized **Task Assignment Dashboard** to communicate employee responsibilities, monitor progress, and maintain accountability.

Every employee is expected to understand their assignments, manage their time responsibly, communicate changes or challenges promptly, and complete work to a high professional standard.

This guide explains how tasks are assigned, how to interpret the Task Assignment Dashboard, and what is expected of employees throughout the working week.

> [!IMPORTANT] Our Commitment to Customers  
> **Our customers and clients are central to everything we do at NASCOMSOFT.**
> 
> The quality of our work, our ability to meet commitments, and our professionalism directly affect customer satisfaction and the company's reputation.
> 
> Every employee is expected to take ownership of their responsibilities and maintain a high standard of work.

---
## 1. Understanding the Task Assignment Dashboard

The [Task Assignment Dashboard](https://nascomsoft.github.io/01-dashboards/tasks-assignment-dashboard/) provides a central overview of employee assignments and their current status.

Tasks are organized under three main headings:

### Overdue / Not Completed

This section contains tasks that have not been completed within their expected timeframe or remain outstanding from previous assignments.

Employees with tasks in this section are expected to understand why the work remains incomplete and be prepared to provide updates.

### In-progress

This section contains tasks that are currently being worked on.

Employees should regularly check this section to identify their active assignments and upcoming deadlines.

### Completed

This section contains tasks that have been reviewed and verified as completed by the Project Manager.

> [!info] Official Task Status  
> Employees do not independently update their task completion status.
> 
> The Project Manager is responsible for reviewing submitted work and updating the dashboard.

---

## 2. How Weekly Task Assignments Are Organized

Within the dashboard, task assignments are grouped by week using callouts that identify the relevant assignment period.

Each weekly callout contains links to the individual task assignment files for employees assigned work during that period.

### How to Find Your Assignment

1. Visit the [Task Assignment Dashboard](https://nascomsoft.github.io/01-dashboards/tasks-assignment-dashboard/).
2. Identify the relevant task status section.
3. Locate the callout for the applicable week.
4. Find the task assignment file bearing your name.
5. Open the file and carefully review the assigned responsibilities.

Your task assignment file may include:

- The week or month of the assignment
- Your name
- The project associated with each task
- Task descriptions and additional instructions
- Dates assigned and expected completion dates
- Task priorities
- Further task breakdowns, where necessary

An employee may have several tasks within the same assignment file, including tasks belonging to different projects.

> [!IMPORTANT] Check Your Assignment Regularly  
> Do not assume that your responsibilities remain unchanged throughout the week.
> 
> New tasks, clarifications, and adjustments may be introduced as project requirements evolve.

---

## 3. How Employees Are Notified of Assignments

The company's general WhatsApp task assignment group is used to communicate updates relating to employee assignments.

When task files are created or updated, the Project Manager may notify employees through this group.

Employees are expected to:

1. Pay attention to task assignment announcements.
2. Open their assignment files after receiving an update.
3. Review any newly added or modified tasks.
4. Take note of deadlines, priorities, and additional instructions.
5. Seek clarification promptly where necessary.

**A WhatsApp announcement is a notification to check your assignment, not a replacement for reading the task file.**

Employees should also check their task files regularly, even when no new announcement has been made.

## 4. Tasks Assigned Outside the Weekly Assignment File

Although the task assignment file is intended to be the primary record of employee responsibilities, not every task will initially appear there.

Certain assignments may be communicated verbally, through WhatsApp, or directly by company management.

### 4.1 Quick and Informal Tasks

Some tasks are brief, straightforward, or urgent enough that they may be assigned and completed without first being added to the weekly task file.

Employees are still expected to carry out such tasks responsibly and within the communicated timeframe.

Where a quick task has a meaningful effect on project progress, existing deadlines, or other assigned responsibilities, the employee should inform the Project Manager.

### 4.2 Tasks Assigned by the CEO

The Chief Executive Officer (CEO) may assign tasks directly to employees without the Project Manager being aware of the assignment.

When this happens, **the employee receiving the task is responsible for informing the Project Manager**.

The employee should communicate:

- The task that was assigned
- The project or activity involved
- The expected deadline
- Any relevant instructions
- Whether the new assignment affects existing tasks

This allows the Project Manager to update the employee's task assignment file where appropriate and maintain an accurate overview of ongoing work.

> [!IMPORTANT] Management Assignments  
> Tasks assigned directly by the CEO are valid assignments and must be treated seriously.
> 
> However, employees must also ensure that the Project Manager is informed so the company's central task records remain accurate.

### 4.3 Impromptu and Urgent Assignments

Project requirements and customer needs may occasionally require tasks to be assigned unexpectedly during the week.

These assignments may take priority over previously scheduled activities, depending on their urgency.

When an impromptu task is assigned:

1. Understand what is required and when it must be completed.
2. Clarify its priority relative to your existing assignments.
3. Inform the Project Manager if the assignment was received from someone else.
4. Identify whether it will affect any existing deadlines.
5. Communicate any scheduling conflicts promptly.
6. Carry out the assignment according to the agreed priorities.

**Do not silently abandon an existing task because a new assignment has been introduced.** Ensure that any conflict in priorities or deadlines is communicated.

## 5. Verbal Instructions and WhatsApp Clarifications

Not every detail of a task can always be captured in its original written description.

Additional information may be communicated:

- Verbally during discussions
- Directly through WhatsApp
- During project meetings
- Through conversations with previous project contributors

These instructions and clarifications can be important for completing a task correctly.

However, the company's goal is to maintain the **task assignment file as the primary source of truth**.

### Employee Responsibilities

When significant new instructions are received:

1. Confirm that you understand the information provided.
2. Identify whether the instructions change the task's scope, deadline, priority, or expected deliverables.
3. Notify the Project Manager if the changes are not already reflected in your task assignment.
4. Refer to the updated task file once the information has been recorded.

Minor clarifications do not necessarily require a formal update. However, important changes affecting accountability, deadlines, or deliverables should be documented.

> [!TIP] Avoid Misunderstandings  
> When an instruction is given verbally and could be misunderstood, briefly confirm your understanding with the person who gave it.
> 
> This is especially important when the instruction changes what you are expected to deliver.

## 6. Deadlines and Time Management

**All assigned deadlines must be taken seriously and strictly adhered to.**

At NASCOMSOFT, one employee's work may depend on another employee completing their assigned responsibilities.

A missed deadline can therefore delay other tasks, affect the overall project schedule, and ultimately affect the customer.

### Employee Expectations

- Review the deadline for every assigned task.
- Plan your work to meet the required completion date.
- Prioritize urgent and high-priority assignments appropriately.
- Avoid unnecessary delays and distractions.
- Inform the Project Manager as soon as you become aware of a possible delay.
- Explain how a new assignment may affect existing commitments.

> [!IMPORTANT] Deadlines Affect Everyone  
> Missing a deadline does not always affect only the employee responsible for the task.
> 
> It may prevent colleagues from proceeding with their work, delay project delivery, and negatively affect customer satisfaction.
> 
> Employees are expected to respect deadlines and take responsibility for meeting them.

If a deadline becomes unrealistic because of a technical problem, missing resource, or conflicting assignment, raise the issue promptly.

Do not wait until the deadline has passed before reporting the problem.

## 7. Working Hours and Professional Conduct

NASCOMSOFT's standard working hours are:

|Day|Working Hours|
|---|---|
|Monday – Friday|9:00 AM – 6:00 PM|

Employees are expected to use company working hours responsibly and remain focused on their assigned duties.

### Personal Activities During Working Hours

Employees must not abandon company assignments or leave the workplace during working hours to attend to personal work without authorization.

Company responsibilities must take priority during official working hours.

Personal work should generally be postponed until after closing time at **6:00 PM**.

Where personal activities are permitted after assigned work has been completed, they must not interfere with other company responsibilities, new assignments, or the employee's availability during working hours.

Employees who need to attend to an important personal matter during working hours should seek the appropriate authorization before leaving or interrupting their duties.

> [!IMPORTANT] Unauthorized Absence and Personal Work  
> Leaving company work to pursue personal activities during official working hours without authorization is unacceptable.
> 
> Such conduct may result in a formal query or other action under applicable company procedures.

### Professional Standards

Every employee is expected to:

- Remain accountable for assigned responsibilities.
- Use working hours productively.
- Maintain a high standard of workmanship.
- Communicate respectfully with colleagues and management.
- Act in the best interests of the company and its customers.
- Avoid conduct that unnecessarily delays or disrupts company operations.

## 8. Handling Company Tools, Equipment, and Resources

Some assignments require the use of physical resources such as:

- Electronic components
- Hardware development boards
- Testing equipment
- Measuring instruments
- Hand tools
- Cables, adapters, and accessories
- Other company equipment or materials

Employees are responsible for handling these resources carefully and ensuring that they remain available for others.

### Resource Handling Guidelines

1. Use equipment only for its intended and authorized purpose.
2. Handle tools and devices carefully to avoid unnecessary damage.
3. Keep track of resources taken for a task.
4. Report damaged, faulty, or missing equipment promptly.
5. Clean and organize the workspace after completing the work.
6. **Return reusable tools and equipment to their appropriate storage locations when finished.**

Do not leave shared equipment scattered around the workplace or retain it unnecessarily when other employees may need it.

> [!IMPORTANT] Shared Resources  
> Company tools and equipment are provided to support everyone's work.
> 
> Failing to return them properly may delay another employee's task and affect project progress.

## 9. Reporting Progress, Challenges, and Completed Work

Employees are expected to communicate relevant updates about their assignments.

### Reporting Challenges

If you encounter a problem that affects your work:

1. Identify the issue.
2. Explain how it affects the task.
3. Describe any steps already taken to resolve it.
4. Inform the Project Manager promptly.
5. Request clarification or assistance where necessary.

For technical questions about existing project work, consult the relevant Project Brief to identify previous contributors who may be able to provide additional information.

### Reporting Completed Work

Once a task has been completed:

1. Prepare the required deliverables.
2. Submit them through the appropriate company platform.
3. Notify the Project Manager.
4. Respond to any feedback or correction requests.

Non-code deliverables are generally submitted through the designated project submissions folder on pCloud, while source code is maintained in the appropriate company GitHub repository.

For more information, read [[03-Vault/Documentation/Project Management/submitting completed work\|Submitting Completed Work]].

> [!info] Task Completion  
> A task is officially completed only after the Project Manager has reviewed and verified the required deliverables.
> 
> Employees should not assume that submitting work automatically changes its status to completed.

## 10. Monday Task Review and Accountability

NASCOMSOFT conducts weekly task review meetings on **Mondays** to evaluate work completed during the previous week and discuss outstanding assignments.

Employees are expected to attend prepared to account for their assigned responsibilities.

During the meeting, employees may be asked to:

- Explain what they accomplished.
- Demonstrate completed work where applicable.
- Identify deliverables submitted for review.
- Discuss incomplete or overdue tasks.
- Explain reasons for delays.
- Describe challenges encountered.
- Provide updates on ongoing assignments.

Employees should be ready to provide clear explanations supported by their work and relevant deliverables.

The purpose of these meetings is to maintain accountability, identify problems, and improve project coordination.

> [!IMPORTANT] Be Prepared  
> Employees are expected to know the status of their assigned tasks and be able to explain their progress.
> 
> Where work remains incomplete, be prepared to discuss the reasons and the steps required to finish it.

## 11. Frequently Asked Questions

**Where can I find my weekly tasks?**

Open the Task Assignment Dashboard, locate the relevant weekly callout, and select the task assignment file bearing your name.

**Will every task appear in my task assignment file?**

Not necessarily. Quick or urgent tasks may initially be communicated verbally or through WhatsApp. Important assignments and changes should be brought to the Project Manager's attention so the central record can be kept accurate.

**What if the CEO assigns me a task directly?**

Carry out the assignment as instructed and inform the Project Manager, including the task details, deadline, and any effect on your existing responsibilities.

**What if I receive an urgent task while working on another assignment?**

Clarify its priority and communicate any conflict with existing deadlines. Do not assume that your earlier responsibilities have been cancelled.

**What if my task instructions change through WhatsApp or a verbal discussion?**

Follow the clarified instructions and notify the Project Manager if the changes affect the task's scope, deadline, priority, or deliverables.

**What if I cannot meet a deadline?**

Notify the Project Manager as soon as you identify the problem. Explain the reason and what is needed to resolve it.

**Can I leave work to handle personal business during working hours?**

Not without appropriate authorization. Company duties take priority during official working hours, and unauthorized absence may result in a formal query.

**What should I do with tools or equipment after completing my task?**

Return reusable tools and equipment to their designated locations and leave the workspace properly organized.

**Who confirms that my task is completed?**

The Project Manager, after reviewing and verifying the required deliverables.
